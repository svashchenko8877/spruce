Spruce – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Data we collect

Spruce does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. Everything Spruce does happens on your Mac.

Files and folders

To find junk and duplicates, Spruce asks you to grant access to your home folder (and optionally the Applications folder) through the standard macOS file dialog. File names, sizes and contents are read locally to find unneeded files and are never uploaded. Items are removed only when you choose to clean them; most go to the Trash.

Subscription

Spruce Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription with a 7-day free trial, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Manage or cancel it anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children

Spruce collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact
