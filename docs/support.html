Spruce – Support

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue at github.com/svashchenko8877/spruce/issues and include your Mac model and macOS version.

Spruce says it needs access

Click Grant Access, choose your home folder in the dialog and press Grant Access. You can change this in Spruce > Settings.

Restore or manage your subscription

Use Restore purchases in Settings, or manage it at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
